A bike computer that measures why you're slow. Record a ride or a roll-down test and it solves for your rolling resistance (**Crr**) and aerodynamic drag (**CdA**), with a confidence signal attached. Change a tyre or your position and get a number back; a server re-fits every upload with real weather.
